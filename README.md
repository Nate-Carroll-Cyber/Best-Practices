## Author
Nate Carroll is an AI security expert, specializing in AI incident response, MLSecOps, and countering AI-enabled disinformation. Backed by nearly 17 years of experience as an Information Security Architect securing Fortune 500, Department of Defense, and Intelligence Community networks, Carroll brings a uniquely battle-tested operational edge to machine learning defense. As a former Counterintelligence Special Agent, he bridges hands-on ML security engineering with strategic adversarial threat modeling. Through his foundational research papers—including *Countering AI-Enabled Disinformation*, the *ODESSA AI Incident Response Loop*, and *Algorithmic Subversion & Strategic Deception*—he pioneers novel frameworks to protect AI pipelines, retrieval systems, and human audiences from sophisticated manipulation.
